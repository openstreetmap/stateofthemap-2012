Pre-conference drinks September 5th

The Japanese local team has found a great place for the already legendary pre-conference drinks: HUB, British Pub near Sjinjuku station.

Check out their website. They even have Guinness 🙂

Be careful: There are four HUB’s near that station, be sure to go to the right one, here are the coordinates on OSM.

This is not a sponsored event so buy your fellow mappers a drink!

Hope to see you there September 5th, around 8pm!
